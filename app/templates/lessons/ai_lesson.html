LESSON CONTENT

What is Artificial Intelligence?

Definition

Artificial Intelligence, commonly called AI, is a branch of computer science that enables machines to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence.

Simple Explanation

AI allows computers to learn from information, recognize patterns, understand language, solve problems and make decisions.

Examples of AI

  • Voice assistants such as Siri and Google Assistant
  • Face recognition systems
  • Recommendation systems on YouTube and Netflix
  • Self-driving vehicle technologies
  • AI chatbots

Applications of AI

Artificial Intelligence is used in many areas including education, healthcare, transportation, banking, agriculture and entertainment.

Key Points

  • AI enables machines to perform intelligent tasks.
  • AI can learn from data.
  • AI can recognize patterns and make predictions.
  • AI is used in many real-world applications.

Real-World Example

When YouTube recommends videos based on the videos you have previously watched, AI analyzes your viewing patterns and suggests content that may be relevant to you.

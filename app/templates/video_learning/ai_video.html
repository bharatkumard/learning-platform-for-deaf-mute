Key Learning Outcome: Understand the basic concepts of Artificial Intelligence, how AI systems work, and how they are used in everyday life.

What is Artificial Intelligence?

AI enables computer systems to perform tasks involving aspects of human intelligence.

📥

Input

The system receives information such as text, images, audio, or other data.

🧠

Process

The AI system analyzes information and looks for useful patterns.

💡

Predict

The system may use what it has learned to make a prediction or recommendation.

✅

Output

The system presents a result, prediction, recommendation, or other output.

Key Concepts

Review these important terms before taking the quiz.

🤖

Artificial Intelligence

Technology that enables machines to perform tasks involving aspects of human intelligence.

🧠

Machine Learning

A field of AI in which systems learn patterns from data.

📊

Data

Information that an AI system can use for analysis or learning.

⚙️

Algorithm

A set of steps or rules used to solve a problem or process information.

Real-World Example: Recommendations

How a recommendation system may suggest content.

1

Quick Review

Remember these points before answering the questions.

  • AI systems can perform tasks involving aspects of human intelligence.
  • AI can process information such as images, audio, and text.
  • Machine Learning is a field of Artificial Intelligence.
  • AI systems can analyze data and produce predictions or outputs.
  • Recommendation systems are one example of AI applications.
